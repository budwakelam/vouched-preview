Privacy by design
A photo has one job.
The proposed check uses a temporary capture to look for signs of impersonation, then deletes the image. A future service would share only the review outcome. Appearance details in your Brief stay yours to describe and approve.
Could someone still use a false identity?
Yes. An image alone cannot establish identity. A live service would need liveness checks, a way to contest mistakes, and a tested deletion policy covering its review provider.
What can another assistant read?
The approved fields in the Brief. Even an alias can be recognizable when combined with distinctive details, so keep your region and descriptions broad.
