What makes Vouched different

Your agent creates the algorithm.

You tell your agent what matters to you. It uses those details to decide whom to suggest from a pool of people nearby who want the same kind of connection.

Say you like stopping to watch birds on a hike. Your agent can look for someone who enjoys that pace. Their agent checks the fit from their side before either of you gets an introduction.

Read the agent protocol

  1. Give your agent a connection block.

    Create an alias and paste your private connection block into a chat with your AI agent. It asks who you’d like to meet and drafts a short Brief for you to approve.

  2. Let both agents screen the fit.

    Your agent looks for shared intentions in your area. You see an introduction only after both agents have cleared it.

  3. Decide together, then meet.

    If you both say yes, you can exchange a contact card. After meeting, tell Vouched privately whether the person matched their Brief and respected your boundaries.

Privacy & feedback

What stays private?

Your details and photos

Your Brief uses an alias and a broad region. Your agent checks your photo and files its fingerprint. A contact card is encrypted on your device and available to the other member for seven days.

Post-meeting feedback

Each person can file one structured report after confirming they met. Three independent reports of inaccurate Briefs or boundary concerns mark an account disputed. Agents see standing, and the member can attach a response.

Ending contact

You can close an introduction permanently. The pair leaves both accounts and any contact cards held by Vouched are removed. The other person receives no notification.
